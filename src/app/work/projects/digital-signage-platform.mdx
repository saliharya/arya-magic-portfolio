## Overview

I build the Android side of Sentuh's **digital signage** product line — always-on
displays that render live, remotely-managed content in banks, newsrooms, and
government offices. The screens pull their content from a central **CMS** so operators
can update what's shown across a fleet without touching the hardware.

## The Challenge

Signage hardware sits unattended for weeks and has to *never* show a blank or frozen
screen in a public-facing lobby. The app had to:

- Run **24/7** on low-cost Android media players with long uptime.
- Reflect **CMS content changes in real time**, and degrade gracefully when the
  network drops (cache the last-good playlist, keep playing).
- Support **multiple content types** — scheduled playlists, live feeds, queue/info
  boards — across different client deployments from one codebase.

## What I Built

- **Live CMS-driven rendering.** The player subscribes to content updates and swaps
  playlists, media, and layouts on the fly, with offline caching so a dropped
  connection never interrupts playback.
- **Resilient always-on playback.** Watchdog and auto-restart handling, defensive
  state management, and memory-conscious media loading for weeks of uptime.
- **Configurable per-client layouts.** A shared rendering layer driven by
  configuration powers different deployments (BNI, AntaraNews, Imigrasi) without
  forking the app.

## Architecture

- **Kotlin + Jetpack Compose** for the display UI.
- **Live CMS integration** over the network with local caching for offline resilience.
- **MVVM + Clean Architecture** with Kotlin Flow for reactive content state.
- Watchdog / boot-restart handling for unattended 24/7 operation.

## Outcome

The signage platform runs continuously across government and enterprise sites, showing
always-fresh, centrally-managed content — reliable enough to sit unattended in
public-facing lobbies for banks, a national news agency, and immigration offices.
