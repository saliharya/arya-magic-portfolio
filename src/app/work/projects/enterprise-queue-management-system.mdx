## Overview

Sentuh's kiosk platform powers self-service terminals, staff displays, and back-office
systems across **7 product lines**. Its flagship healthcare deployment is a
self-service **Queue Management System (QMS)** kiosk at **Medistra Hospital**, serving
**11,000+ patients per month** for registration, returning-patient lookup, doctor
selection, appointment booking, and queue check-in — all without staff assistance.

## The Challenge

Queueing is unforgiving: a frozen kiosk or a stale "now serving" number directly
becomes a line of frustrated people — many of them elderly or unwell. The system had to:

- Stay correct in **real time** — every ticket issued or called had to propagate to
  all screens instantly.
- Run **24/7 with zero downtime**, including graceful recovery from flaky on-site networks.
- Be usable by **elderly and unwell patients** with zero training.
- Scale across **multiple product lines** without forking the codebase for each one.

## What I Built

- **Real-time sync over WebSocket.** Replaced polling with a persistent WebSocket
  channel so kiosks, displays, and the backend share a single live source of truth.
  Implemented automatic reconnection with backoff and state re-hydration so a
  dropped connection never leaves a screen showing stale data.
- **A strict single-flow-per-patient UX.** Large touch targets, minimal steps, no
  branching dead-ends, and a linear progression from identification to a printed
  queue ticket — deliberately optimized for elderly users and patients who aren't
  feeling well.
- **Two patient journeys.** Returning patients look up records via medical-record
  number, NIK, or name + date of birth; new walk-ins register fresh — each routing
  through doctor-specialty selection, schedule browsing, time-slot picking, and
  payment-method confirmation before queue registration.
- **Resilient, always-on Android clients.** Defensive state handling, offline
  buffering, and idempotent message processing to avoid double-counting tickets
  across restarts and long uptime.

## Architecture

- **Kotlin + Jetpack Compose** for the kiosk and display apps.
- **MVI (Model-View-Intent)** with a `MutableStateFlow` sealed `UiState` per
  ViewModel — every action (loading, success, error) maps to a distinct typed state
  variant, making transitions explicit, testable, and free of implicit side-effects.
- **Clean Architecture with hard module boundaries:** `:domain` has zero Android
  dependencies and owns business logic via use cases and repository interfaces;
  `:data` owns Retrofit, observables, and response-to-domain mappers; `:app` owns UI
  and consumes domain use cases through **Dagger Hilt** injection.
- **Single-Activity architecture** with Jetpack Navigation routing all fragments
  through one shared ViewModel, keeping patient context consistent and automatically
  cleared after each session completes.
- **Dynamic base URL** via an OkHttp interceptor lets admins switch environments from
  the kiosk settings screen without redeploying.
- **WebSocket** for real-time bidirectional state sync across the fleet.

## Outcome

The platform runs continuously in production, moving **11,000+ patients per month**
through queues at Medistra Hospital with **zero-downtime** real-time updates. The
WebSocket design eliminated the lag and stale-state issues of the previous polling
approach, and the configuration-driven, single-flow UI lets new product lines and
deployments launch without a separate build.
